A deck-building battler

Every copy starts the same.
Yours won't stay that way.

Twelve creatures, none of them levelled. Win a battle and they earn growth points — you decide which stats each one keeps. Push a card far enough and it is promoted to a higher power tier for good, which makes it stronger and more expensive to field. Five battles stand between you and the Hollow Crown.

Rarity is headroom

Common through Mythic adds no stats. It sets how many levels and growth points a card will ever get.

Tier is right now

Weak, Normal, Elite, Legendary, Ascendant. Read off the card's power score, so levelling promotes it.

Growth costs tempo

A higher tier hits harder and costs more energy to deploy. Levelling everything is a real decision.

Focus is yours

Might, Vitality, Speed, Guard. A point is worth the same power score in any of them — the creature is what changes.

Creatures can't attack the round they land. Front slots are hit first.
