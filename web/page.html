A deck-building duel

Every copy starts the same.
Yours will not stay that way.

Ten creatures and six effects, none of them levelled. You hold three cards. You choose what comes down, which creature swings, and what it swings at. Every attack costs a creature stamina — spend the last of it and the card is done, back to the discard pile for something fresh.

Three cards, always

Your hand refills to three at the start of your turn. Spent and slain creatures cycle back through the deck.

Stamina is a clock

Each attack spends one. A common has more uses than a mythic — big cards hit harder and leave sooner.

Lanes open the nexus

Attack any enemy creature you like. Strike the nexus only when the lane facing your attacker is empty.

Levelling is yours to aim

Win, and your creatures earn growth points, and every fourth level a card offers two paths. The one you take is permanent.

The deck grows with you

After each duel you draft a card from the whole set, or cut one loose. Recruits arrive at the level of the warband they join.

The route is a choice

Twelve rows of branching paths. Elites hit harder and pay better, waystones buy you levels, caches buy you cards. You cannot take them all.

Twelve rows, twenty-eight things living on them, and one crown at the top.

Play a card

Click a card in hand, then click one of your three lanes to place a creature. Effects ask for their target instead.

Attack

Click one of your ready creatures, then click an enemy. A creature cannot attack the turn it lands, and swings once per turn.

Stamina

The pips under a creature are its remaining uses. At zero it is spent and returns to your discard pile.

The nexus

Reduce the enemy nexus to zero. It can only be struck through a lane with no enemy creature facing your attacker.
